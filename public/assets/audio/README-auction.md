# Auction audio

auction-crowd.mp3 is the high-quality preview of “Busy Room Ambience / People talking in background #2”, by Breviceps, CC0.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/465699/
Audio URL: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/465/465699_9159316-hq.mp3
Downloaded 2026-10-13. Unmodified recording of people talking in an auditorium.

auction-three-gavels.wav is original synthesized wooden-impact audio with three strikes at 0, 0.9 and 1.8 seconds. Regenerate with node scripts/generateAuctionGavel.mjs.

The crowd follows the room state, fades in and out, and continues after the sale. Master mute and volume apply to both tracks. Gavel audio is triggered once in the last three seconds of the auction.

## Sale reaction

- auction-applause.mp3: “Applause 14” by Sadiquecat, CC0, https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/806758/
